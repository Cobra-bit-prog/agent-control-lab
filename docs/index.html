The check

Before money moves, the agent asks. Agent Control answers fine, wait, or stop.

{
  "to": "<destination>",
  "value_usd": 12,
  "job_id": "job_research"
}

Header: Authorization: Bearer YOUR_AGENT_API_KEY. Lab demo key: ack_lab_leashed.

Fine: within the job cap, within the daily cap, known payee — then settle. Wait: a new payee waits on you. Stop: over a cap, or the job has ended — do not send.

When a service asks for USDC

Sit on the pay loop. Do not replace it.

  1. 1. 402 — the service asks for USDC.
  2. 2. POST /api/v1/check — same body as any other pay. MCP check_transfer maps here. Not a second check.
  3. 3. fine / wait / stop — you keep the keys.
  4. 4. Only then settle — if the answer was fine. Wait means a human still has to see the new address. Stop means do not send.

A facilitator fee may be added later. Not charged now.
