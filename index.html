Questions operators ask

Do you hold my keys?

No. We return allow, hold, or abort. You still sign. If the agent skips the hook, we cannot physically stop the chain.

How do I pay? Is there KYC?

No KYC and no card. Default is USDC. Scan the QR or copy amount + address. Do not send from an exchange — they drop the memo / unique dust.

After 24 hours?

Dashboard locks until you pay. The hook returns abort so a leftover agent cannot keep spending. Renewal reminder 7 days before month end. No autopay.

Hold vs block?

Hold waits for a human: Allow once, Always allow, or Block. Block is a hard no (paused agent or denylist).
